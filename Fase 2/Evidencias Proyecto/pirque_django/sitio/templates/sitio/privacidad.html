At {{ SITIO.nombre }} we take care of your personal data. This policy explains what data we ask for, why, and how you can exercise your rights under Chilean Law 21,719 on Personal Data Protection.

1. Who is responsible for your data

{{ SITIO.nombre }}, {{ SITIO.direccion }}. Contact: {{ SITIO.email }} · WhatsApp +{{ SITIO.whatsapp }}.

2. What data we ask for

  • To request a booking: first name, last name, RUT or ID/passport number, email, phone, dates, number of guests and the language of the website.
  • Contact form: name, email and your message.
  • Reviews: rating, comment and the public name you choose.

We only ask for what is necessary. We do not ask for card details or charge online.

3. What we use it for

To manage your request and your stay, to contact you by email or WhatsApp, to confirm or cancel your booking, to invite you to leave a review after your stay and to meet our legal obligations. We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Legal basis

Your explicit consent, given by ticking the box “I accept the Privacy Policy”, and the management of the booking you request. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.

5. Who can see your data

Only the staff of the lodging, through an administration panel protected by a password. The services we need to operate (website hosting and email) process the data on our behalf. If you choose to write to us on WhatsApp, your message is also subject to WhatsApp's terms.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to manage your booking and to meet our legal and tax obligations. After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification, deletion, portability or blocking, and object to its processing. Write to {{ SITIO.email }} with your name and RUT/ID; we will answer within the time limits set by law. You may also file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Agency.

8. How we protect it

Encrypted passwords, access only for authorized staff, automatic blocking after repeated failed logins, a record of important changes, regular backups of the database and protection against the most common web attacks.

Last updated: November 2026.
